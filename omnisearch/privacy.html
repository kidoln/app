Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

OmniSearch (the "App") is a local filename search tool for macOS made by Kido Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the App.

We collect nothing.

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting. It makes no network requests of any kind. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data — the App simply has no code that talks to the internet.

Everything stays on your Mac.

To search file names, the App builds a local index containing file names, folder paths, file sizes, and modification timestamps. It also stores your search history and recently opened files so results can be ranked smarter over time. All of this data lives locally on your Mac, inside the App's own storage. It never leaves your device, and it is deleted if you remove the App.

Your files are yours.

The App never reads the contents of your files — only their names and basic metadata. Access to each folder is granted by you through the standard macOS system dialog and can be revoked at any time.

No third parties.

Because the App collects no data and makes no network connections, no third party receives anything from it.

Children.

The App is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy.

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日

OmniSearch（下称「本应用」）是一款由 Kido Studio（下称「我们」）开发的 macOS 本地文件名搜索工具。本政策说明您使用本应用时信息是如何处理的。

我们不收集任何数据。

本应用不含任何分析、广告、追踪或崩溃上报组件，不发起任何网络请求。我们不收集、传输、共享或出售任何个人数据——本应用中根本没有任何联网代码。

一切都保存在您的 Mac 上。

为搜索文件名，本应用在本地建立索引，内容包括文件名、文件夹路径、文件大小与修改时间。此外会保存您的搜索历史与最近打开的文件，以便结果排序越来越智能。所有数据都只存在于您 Mac 上本应用自己的存储空间内，绝不离开您的设备；卸载应用即随之删除。

您的文件属于您。

本应用永不读取文件内容——只访问文件名与基本元数据。对每个文件夹的访问权限都由您通过 macOS 标准系统对话框主动授予，随时可以撤回。

无任何第三方。

由于本应用不收集数据、不联网，任何第三方都不会从它收到任何信息。

儿童。

本应用不面向儿童，也不从任何人处收集信息。

政策变更。

如本政策发生变化，我们会更新本页面。
